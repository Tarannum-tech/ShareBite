How It Works

Our simple process connects food donors with local charities in just a few clicks

Food is Donated

Restaurants, grocery stores, and farms post their excess food on our platform with just a few taps.

Charities Get Notified

Local organizations receive instant alerts about available food donations in their area.

Food is Delivered

Charities or volunteers pick up the food and distribute it to those who need it most.
