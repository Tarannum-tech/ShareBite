Why Donate Food?

Every year, millions of pounds of perfectly good food go to waste while many in our community struggle with hunger. By donating your excess food, you can:

  • Help feed families in need
  • Reduce food waste and environmental impact
  • Support local charities and community organizations
  • Potentially qualify for tax deductions (consult your tax advisor)

Food Donation Guidelines

We accept most types of food, but please follow these guidelines:

  • Food must be unopened and in original packaging when possible
  • Prepared foods must be properly stored and within safe time limits
  • No home-canned or home-prepared foods (except from licensed kitchens)
  • No expired or spoiled food items

How It Works

  1. Fill out the donation form with details about your food
  2. We'll match you with a nearby charity or volunteer
  3. Schedule a convenient pickup time
  4. Our team will collect the food and deliver it to those in need

For large or recurring donations, please contact us to discuss special arrangements.

About Us

Food Rescue Network is a nonprofit organization dedicated to reducing food waste and hunger by connecting food donors with local charities.
