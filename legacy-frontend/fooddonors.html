Benefits of Donating

Partnering with Food Rescue Network provides multiple benefits for your business

Reduce Waste

Lower your disposal costs and environmental impact by diverting food from landfills

Tax Deductions

Receive documentation for tax-deductible donations under the Bill Emerson Good Samaritan Act

Community Impact

Strengthen your ties to the community and demonstrate your commitment to social responsibility

How Donating Works

Our simple process makes food donation easy and convenient

1

Sign Up

Register your business in our system and complete a brief orientation

2

Post Donations

Use our app or website to list available food when you have surplus

3

We Handle the Rest

Our network of charities and volunteers will arrange pickup at your convenience

Foods We Accept

We rescue a wide variety of food items that meet safety standards

Prepared Foods

  • Hot/cold buffet items
  • Catering leftovers
  • Bakery goods
  • Pre-packaged meals
  • Deli items

Perishable Goods

  • Fresh produce
  • Dairy products
  • Meat & poultry
  • Seafood
  • Eggs

Non-Perishable Items

  • Canned goods
  • Dry pasta & rice
  • Cereals & grains
  • Packaged snacks
  • Bottled beverages
