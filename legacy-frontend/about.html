Who We Are

We are a team of three passionate students from LTCE, driven by the vision of leveraging technology for social good. Recognizing the critical issue of food wastage alongside the growing needs of underserved communities, we created ShareBit—a comprehensive platform that seamlessly connects volunteers, nonprofit organizations, and individuals in need.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between surplus food and those who need it most by providing an efficient, transparent, and accessible solution for food donation. Through ShareBit, we aim to empower individuals and organizations to contribute meaningfully, reduce food waste, and foster a culture of giving.

By creating a collaborative ecosystem, we aspire to make a lasting impact on communities, ensuring that no meal goes to waste and that every individual has access to essential nourishment.
